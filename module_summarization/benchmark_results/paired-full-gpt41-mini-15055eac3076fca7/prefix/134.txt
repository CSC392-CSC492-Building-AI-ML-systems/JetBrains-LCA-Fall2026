Alchemical Transformations
===========================

Overview
--------

Alchemical transformations are a powerful technique used in molecular simulations to compute free energy differences between two states, such as different molecular species or conformations. This approach involves gradually transforming one molecular system into another by modifying the system's Hamiltonian through a coupling parameter, commonly denoted as \(\lambda\), which varies from 0 to 1.

In the context of alchemical transformations, atoms and their interactions are classified into distinct groups based on their presence and mapping between the initial (old) and final (new) states. This classification enables the systematic scaling and modification of interactions during the transformation.

Atom Classification
-------------------

Atoms in the hybrid system are categorized into four classes:

- **unique_old_atom**  
  Atoms that exist only in the old system and have no counterpart in the new system. Their interactions are fully active at \(\lambda=0\) and are turned off as \(\lambda\) approaches 1.

- **unique_new_atom**  
  Atoms that exist only in the new system and have no counterpart in the old system. Their interactions are off at \(\lambda=0\) and gradually turned on as \(\lambda\) approaches 1.

- **core_atom**  
  Atoms that are mapped between the old and new systems and belong to residues undergoing change. Their interactions interpolate between the old system at \(\lambda=0\) and the new system at \(\lambda=1\).

- **environment_atom**  
  Atoms that are mapped but not part of the changing residues. Their interactions remain constant and are not modified alchemically.

Hybrid Topology Factory
-----------------------

The `HybridTopologyFactory` class facilitates the creation of a hybrid topology that represents the alchemical transformation between two molecular states. It takes as input a topology proposal describing the mapping between old and new systems, along with their respective atomic positions.

Key features of the `HybridTopologyFactory` include:

- Generation of a hybrid OpenMM system that incorporates both old and new atoms and their interactions.
- Maintenance of mappings between old/new atoms and their corresponding atoms in the hybrid system.
- Support for softcore potentials to avoid singularities during the transformation.
- Customizable scaling functions for bonds, angles, torsions, sterics, and electrostatics as functions of \(\lambda\).
- Options to soften bonded interactions involving unique atoms to improve numerical stability.
- Handling of neglected angle terms that may arise from geometry engine approximations.
- Support for advanced features such as dispersion corrections, interpolation of 1-4 interactions, and RMSD restraints.

Parameters and Usage
--------------------

When initializing a `HybridTopologyFactory`, several parameters can be specified to control the alchemical transformation:

- **topology_proposal**: A `TopologyProposal` object that encodes the mapping between old and new topologies.
- **current_positions** and **new_positions**: Atomic coordinates for the old and new systems, respectively.
- **use_dispersion_correction**: Whether to include long-range dispersion corrections in the sterics force.
- **functions**: A dictionary specifying how each force component scales with \(\lambda\). Keys should correspond to force types such as bonds, angles, torsions, sterics, and electrostatics.
- **softcore_alpha**: Parameter controlling the softness of the softcore sterics potential.
- **bond_softening_constant** and **angle_softening_constant**: Factors to soften bonded interactions involving unique atoms.
- **neglected_new_angle_terms** and **neglected_old_angle_terms**: Lists of angle indices that require special treatment due to geometry engine limitations.
- **softcore_LJ_v2**, **softcore_electrostatics**, and related parameters: Flags and parameters controlling the implementation of softcore potentials for Lennard-Jones and electrostatic interactions.
- **interpolate_old_and_new_14s**: Whether to interpolate 1-4 interactions between old and new states.
- **flatten_torsions** and **rmsd_restraint**: Additional options for modifying torsion potentials and applying RMSD restraints during the transformation.

Usage of the `HybridTopologyFactory` enables rigorous and flexible alchemical free energy calculations by providing a consistent hybrid representation of the molecular systems involved.

Notes
-----

- The API is experimental and subject to change.
- Proper selection and tuning of softcore parameters and scaling functions are critical for stable and accurate alchemical transformations.
- Logging is available to monitor the transformation process and diagnose potential issues.
