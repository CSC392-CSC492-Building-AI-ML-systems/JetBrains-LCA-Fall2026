Alchemical Transformations
==========================

Overview
--------

Alchemical transformations are a powerful technique used in molecular simulations to compute free energy differences between two states, such as different molecular species or conformations. This approach involves constructing a hybrid system that smoothly interpolates between an initial ("old") system and a final ("new") system by gradually turning on and off interactions through a coupling parameter, commonly denoted as lambda (λ).

Hybrid Topology Factory
-----------------------

The core component enabling alchemical transformations in this framework is the **HybridTopologyFactory** class. This class generates a hybrid topology and system based on a proposed transformation between two molecular systems. It manages the mapping of atoms and interactions between the old and new systems and constructs a hybrid OpenMM system suitable for alchemical free energy calculations.

Atom Classes
~~~~~~~~~~~

Atoms in the hybrid system are classified into four distinct groups, each treated differently during the alchemical transformation:

- **unique_old_atom**: Atoms present only in the old system and not mapped to the new system. Their interactions are fully active at λ=0 and turned off at λ=1.
- **unique_new_atom**: Atoms present only in the new system and not mapped to the old system. Their interactions are off at λ=0 and fully active at λ=1.
- **core_atom**: Atoms that are mapped between old and new systems and belong to residues undergoing change. Their interactions interpolate between old and new states as λ varies from 0 to 1.
- **environment_atom**: Atoms mapped between old and new systems but not part of the changing residues. Their interactions remain constant and are not alchemically modified.

Key Features and Parameters
---------------------------

- **Softcore Potentials**: To avoid singularities and numerical instabilities during the transformation, softcore potentials are employed for Lennard-Jones and electrostatic interactions. Parameters such as `softcore_alpha`, `softcore_LJ_v2_alpha`, and `softcore_electrostatics_alpha` control the softness of these potentials.

- **Force Scaling Functions**: Custom lambda-dependent scaling functions can be provided to control how different force terms (bonds, angles, torsions, sterics, electrostatics) are modulated during the alchemical process.

- **Bond and Angle Softening**: Force constants for bonds and angles involving unique atoms can be softened to facilitate smoother transformations.

- **Handling of Exceptions**: Special care is taken to manage exceptions in nonbonded interactions, ensuring that interactions involving unique and environment atoms are treated consistently.

- **Constraints and Virtual Sites**: Constraints from the original systems are preserved if they do not involve changing atoms. Virtual sites are supported only in the environment region and are copied to the hybrid system.

- **RMSD Restraints**: Optionally, RMSD restraints can be imposed between core heavy atoms and protein alpha carbons to maintain structural integrity during the transformation.

Workflow Summary
----------------

1. **Initialization**: The factory is initialized with a topology proposal describing the mapping between old and new systems, along with their respective atomic positions.

2. **Particle Addition and Mapping**: All particles from the old system are added to the hybrid system first, followed by unique new atoms. Mappings between old/new and hybrid atom indices are established.

3. **Force Copying and Validation**: Forces from the old and new systems are inspected and validated to ensure compatibility. Known force types include bonds, angles, torsions, nonbonded forces, and barostats.

4. **Hybrid System Construction**: The hybrid system is assembled by copying particles, constraints, virtual sites, and forces, with appropriate modifications to enable alchemical scaling.

5. **Force Term Handling**: Bond, angle, torsion, and nonbonded force terms are added and modified to reflect the alchemical transformation, including softcore potentials and lambda-dependent scaling.

6. **Exception Management**: Nonbonded exceptions are carefully handled to maintain physical consistency and avoid artifacts during the transformation.

7. **Position and Topology Generation**: Hybrid atomic positions are computed, and a corresponding topology object is created for downstream simulation.

Applications
------------

The HybridTopologyFactory and the alchemical transformation framework it supports are essential for:

- Relative binding free energy calculations between ligands.
- Mutational free energy calculations in proteins.
- Solvation free energy computations.
- Any scenario requiring smooth interpolation between molecular states.

Notes
-----

- This API is experimental and subject to change.
- Annealing of omitted terms and certain advanced features are not currently supported.
- Virtual sites in changing residues are not supported.
- Proper validation of input systems and mappings is critical for successful alchemical transformations.

Logging and Debugging
---------------------

The implementation includes detailed logging at the INFO level to trace the construction and validation steps of the hybrid system, aiding in debugging and development.
